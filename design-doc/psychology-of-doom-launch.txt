Research corrections:
Plato's writing criticism is a dialogue and a myth recounted by Socrates, not evidence of a historical anti-book campaign.
Trithemius defended handwritten copying and parchment but also used print distribution.
The 1524 almanac's conjunction forecast was amplified into deluge predictions by other astrologers.
Millerite expectations settled on October 22, 1844; no unsupported ascension-robe anecdotes.
The Ehrlich retrospective acknowledges underestimating the Green Revolution, while hunger and environmental damage remained real.
Y2K was a real software problem addressed by remediation, not simply an imaginary scare.
The LHC safety argument evaluated mechanisms and natural-collision and astrophysical evidence.
Camping's later admission of error is acknowledged.
Maya calendar rollover is distinguished from modern apocalypse marketing.
Psychology findings are not treated as universal laws or proof that any particular concern is false.
