Why technological change feels like catastrophe, how fear becomes a toll booth, and how to build defenses people can own.

Suggested announcement:
Fear can build a useful defense. It can also build a toll booth. Our new essay examines loss aversion, status quo bias, negativity, availability, and perceived decline—and asks what a proposed response actually lets people own.

Editorial grounding:
Read all 15 preceding posts and the About page. The argument follows the archive's emphasis on ownership, open and inspectable tools, practical defenses, abundance that reaches people, and skepticism toward both doom rhetoric and corporate promises. Real displacement and control failures remain real costs. Cognitive bias is not used to dismiss risk. Optimistic forecasts face the same accountability standard.
