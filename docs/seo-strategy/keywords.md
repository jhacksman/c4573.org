# Target Keywords & Long-Tail Opportunities

## Keyword Strategy Overview

c4573.org operates in the intersection of three keyword clusters:
1. **Data scraping tools** (developer audience)
2. **Public records / property data** (real estate investor audience)
3. **Anti-SaaS / cheap alternatives** (price-sensitive buyer audience)

The third cluster is our differentiator. We don't compete with Scrapy or Apify on "web scraping tool" — we compete on "cheap alternative to BatchData" and "scrape texas property records yourself."

## Keyword Research Methodology

Volume estimates are based on:
- Google Trends relative interest data
- Competitor traffic analysis via SimilarWeb public data
- Apify marketplace search volumes for similar tools
- BiggerPockets forum post frequency for property data topics
- Reddit r/realestateinvesting post frequency

These are directional estimates, not exact volumes. For precise data, use Google Keyword Planner, Ahrefs, or SEMrush with a paid account.
