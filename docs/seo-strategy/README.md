**Date:** February 2026
**Status:** Strategy Document
**Author:** c4573.org team

## Executive Summary

c4573.org sells $10 developer tools (data scrapers, automation kits) positioned against $50-$500/month SaaS competitors. The SEO strategy targets real estate investors, AI developers, and indie hackers searching for affordable data extraction tools.

**Core positioning:** "Own your data tools for $10" vs. paying monthly rent to BatchData ($500/mo), PropStream ($99/mo), ATTOM ($850+/mo).

**Primary traffic channels:**
1. Organic search (long-tail keywords with buyer intent)
2. Community marketing (BiggerPockets, Reddit, Hacker News)
3. Gumroad discovery + internal SEO

## Key Strategic Decisions

1. **Target real estate investors, not developers.** Developers expect free tools. RE investors pay $29-$149 for data tools without blinking because it saves them $200+/mo.
2. **Lead with pain point, not product.** "Stop paying $500/month for property data" ranks better and converts better than "Python scraper for sale."
3. **Long-tail first.** Don't compete for "web scraping tool" (dominated by Scrapy, Apify, Bright Data). Compete for "texas property records scraper" and "free texas county assessor data."
4. **Content as acquisition.** Blog posts targeting "how to scrape [county] property records" drive organic traffic that converts to tool purchases.
5. **Gumroad SEO matters.** Gumroad has domain authority (DA 80+). Optimize product listing for Gumroad's internal search and Google indexing of Gumroad pages.
